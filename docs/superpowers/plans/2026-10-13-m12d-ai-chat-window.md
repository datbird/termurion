### Pitfalls for the implementer

- Per-window state: `chat_indicator_hits`, `picker_hits` and `inline_hits` are rebuilt while a window draws; they must be `WinSlot` fields in `swap_live`, or a press in one window tests another window's rectangles. Everything keyed by `PaneId` (`inline`, `streaming_chats`, the picker) is app-wide.
- `gutter_split` must be the only place that takes room from a pane, and it runs before any tmux size report; the panel is refused in tmux panes, so tmux's locked sizes never meet it.
