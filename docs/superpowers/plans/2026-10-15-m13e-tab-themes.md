**Goal:** a person picks a theme in Settings > Appearance > General > Theme and the tab bar looks as iTerm2's does: Regular follows Windows' light, dark and high contrast modes; Light and Dark force that look (and the profile's light or dark colors); the High Contrast themes maximize visibility; colored tabs fill with their color and outline the selected one; Minimal blends the tab bar into the session's background; Minimal and Compact put the tabs in the caption area with the window buttons, drag the window from the bar's edges or a single tab, and can show the window name beside the tabs.

Drawing is `scene.rs` (`tab_bar` and 13d's `tab_bar_vertical` take a `&TabPalette`; colored-tab fill and outline; the Minimal notch; caption buttons; the window name label). The app side: `src/app/caption_ui.rs` (`#[cfg(windows)]` window subclass for `WM_NCCALCSIZE` and `WM_NCHITTEST`, its own `SUBCLASS_ID`, fed by a thread-local caption layout per window) and edits in `src/app/mod.rs`, `src/app/windows_ui.rs`, `src/app/requests_ui.rs`, `src/app/tab_drag_ui.rs` (13d), `src/settings_ui.rs`. ### Pitfalls for the implementer

- `STATUS_BAR_BG`, `STATUS_BAR_TEXT`, `STATUS_BAR_EDGE` alias the TAB_* constants; keep the aliases' values (define them as their own constants) when the tab bar stops using TAB_*.
- The caption subclass runs inside winit's window procedure; it must never borrow `App`. Read the layout from the thread-local map `publish` fills each frame, and answer `DefSubclassProc` when no layout is published.
- `WM_NCCALCSIZE` changes the client size: winit then reports a larger `inner_size`, and `window_room` must start below the caption bar, or panes draw under the caption buttons. tmux sizes follow the pane rects.
- A maximized window with no caption overhangs the monitor by the frame thickness; add it back at the top in `WM_NCCALCSIZE` (and the hit test's `resize_top` is 0 then).
- `App::dark` is read in many places (toolbelt, lists, profile colors); forcing it through one function keeps them consistent. Do not add a second "forced" check anywhere else.
- A theme change must re-theme every open window's type, not just new windows; a window leaving Compact needs its subclass removed and `SWP_FRAMECHANGED`.
