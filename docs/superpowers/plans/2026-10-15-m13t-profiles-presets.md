**Goal:** a person can pick any of iTerm2's eleven color presets (or their own, imported from `.itermcolors`) from the Colors tab and see them previewed and checked exactly as in iTerm2, load a preset from a key binding, Open Quickly or a script, bind a color or a checkbox to an expression, drag colors on the hue and chroma wheel, find profiles by name or tag in Settings and the Profiles window, open several at once, reach tagged profiles through Profiles menu submenus, open a profile with its Ctrl+Win shortcut, and have Edit Session follow whichever session is active.

**Architecture:** pure models first, each a plain `pub mod` tested on EXTREME. `color_presets.rs` is the preset model: the built-in list parsed from iTerm2's `ColorPresets.plist` (copied into `resources/`), custom presets in the global "Custom Color Presets", `.itermcolors` reading and writing, iTerm2's apply rule with light and dark modes, the current-colors dictionary, and the check-mark match. `bindings.rs` is the "Bindings" profile key (plain or palette-owned values), the type help and the value conversions. `oklab.rs` is the wheel's color math and geometry. `profile_list.rs` is the tag hierarchy and the tag query. `profile_menu.rs` builds the Profiles menu's tag tree. `bonjour.rs` is the profile a found host makes, with a `#[cfg(windows)]` `DnsServiceBrowse` browser. Windows (egui): `profile_list_ui.rs` (the search field, list and tag sidebar, shared by Settings and the Profiles window), `hue_wheel_ui.rs`, extensions to `settings_ui.rs`. The app side: `src/app/presets_ui.rs` (loading presets into sessions and profiles), `src/app/bindings_ui.rs` (evaluating bindings per session), `src/app/bonjour_ui.rs`, and the Profiles menu in `windows_ui.rs`. Presets and bindings change profiles and session copies through the store and `App::set_session_values`; the Settings window's edit-session state is app-wide; no new field is per window, so none goes in `swap_live`.
