### Decisions (each goes in `m13l-rulings.txt`)

- Tandem is iTerm2's GPU path for every cadence rule (Maximize throughput decides adaptivity; `MetalSlowFrameRate`; `ActiveUpdateCadence` in the alternate screen); `slowFrameRate` stays n/a. Cost: the non-Metal keys do nothing.
- Only pane output is paced; keys, the mouse, resizes, bars, timers and the cursor redraw at once, as iTerm2's other `setNeedsDisplay` paths do. Cost: a busy pane redrawn by a timer can draw faster than its cadence.
- A window's interval is the smallest over its front tab's visible panes. Cost: a quiet pane beside a busy one follows the busy one (one surface per window; iTerm2 has one view per session).
- Windows battery saver (`SystemStatusFlag` 1, which Windows 11's Energy saver also sets) is iTerm2's Low Power Mode; being on battery alone is not. Cost: a laptop on battery without the saver draws at full rate.
- `disableMetalWhenUnplugged` and `disableMetalInLowPowerMode` are stored and inert and `02:151` is partial (13j-l rulings): Tandem has no CPU renderer to fall back to, and the frame caps already save power. Cost: settings that do nothing.
- Cursor redraws are not paced: `cursor_wake` passes through `about_to_wait`'s `wake` array unchanged, as iTerm2 does not pace them. Cost: none.
- `preferIntegratedGPU`'s enabling uses a process-wide flag set once by `init_gpu` (the settings window has no App). Cost: before the first window's GPU starts the row reads disabled.
- The underline color lives in `Palette` (which knows light or dark), not in the terminal `Look`. Cost: none.
- `preferIntegratedGPU` maps to `PowerPreference::LowPower`, chosen once per run. Cost: a machine whose integrated GPU is weak gets it by default, as iTerm2's users do.
- `DisableAppNap` maps to opting out of EcoQoS (`SetProcessInformation`), applied at start only. Cost: the rest of Windows' power management still applies.
- The FPS meter draws over the focused pane's first row in the window, where iTerm2 draws one per session view. Cost: other panes show no meter.
- Copy Performance Stats copies Tandem's renderer timings (start-to-start, surface wait, dropped frames) under iTerm2's text format, and the histogram summary drops the sparkline glyphs; percentiles interpolate linearly between ranks. Cost: numbers are not comparable with iTerm2's.
- GPU Renderer Availability always says enabled and adds the adapter name and backend on a second paragraph. Cost: none.
- Underline geometry uses the primary face's swash metrics (underline offset, stroke size, x-height, baseline from cosmic-text's line layout) in iTerm2's clamping formulas; macOS's typesetter offset is not available. Cost: underlines may sit a pixel away from iTerm2's for the same font.
- Gaps are cut around each glyph's ink box (widened 1 pixel each side) where it crosses the underline band, not by a per-pixel mask. Cost: a glyph with ink only at one side leaves a wider gap than iTerm2's.
- Strikethrough uses the underline color rules (SGR 58, then the profile's Underline Color, then the text color), as iTerm2's descriptor does. Cost: none (kept quirk).
- Margins below 0 count as 0; iTerm2 does not bound them. Cost: none.
- Reduce Flicker holds the pane's last drawn picture (its slice of the frame), not a grid snapshot drawn again. Cost: a resize during a hold shows the old picture until it ends (at most 0.2 s).
- The OSC 8 dash moves from the cell bottom to the underline position with iTerm2's 4-on, 3-off pattern (R3). Cost: links look different from Tandem's past.

### Pitfalls for the implementer

- `after_output` is also where finds, marks and composer state update; move only the final `redraw()` behind the pacer, never the rest.
- A pane's output while its window is hidden or minimized must not request a frame at all (iTerm2's background cadence only updates the tab label); the tab bar's activity color still updates through its own timer.
- Keep `frame_wake`'s result in `about_to_wait`'s `wake` array; a missing due time leaves output undrawn until the next event.
- `Throughput::estimate` mutates; call it once per pane per decision.
- `render` must record the frame even when the surface texture times out, or the pacer thinks it never drew and spins.
- The underline overlay quads must be trimmed by `cut_out` like text rows, or they draw over popups (the overlay pass is after popups).
- Margins change the pty size: go through `relayout` so ConPTY, tmux and the status bar room are all updated in one place; never resize a pty from a draw.
- Glyph ink boxes come from the swash cache inside `Renderer::draw`; do not rasterize glyphs a second time to find them.
- `SetProcessInformation` needs `PROCESS_POWER_THROTTLING_STATE` sized exactly; pass `size_of::<PROCESS_POWER_THROTTLING_STATE>() as u32`.
