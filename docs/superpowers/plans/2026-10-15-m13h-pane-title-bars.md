**Goal:** a person with split panes sees a title bar on each pane exactly as in iTerm2 (on by default), closes, maximizes and opens the pane's menu from it (script context menu items included), and moves or swaps panes by the Move Session to Split Pane pick, by the key and pointer actions, or by dragging a title bar onto another pane's half, in any window, tmux panes included.

**Architecture:** pure models first, each a plain `pub mod` tested on the Windows test machine. `hexcolor.rs` is iTerm2's `colorFromHexString:` and the color math title bars need (brightness, dimming, HSB). `pane_title.rs` is the title bar model: when a tab shows title bars, the ordinals, the title text, the geometry of every part (close, label, lock, menu, the status bar slot, the bottom line) and the colors. `pane_move.rs` is the pick and drag model: the half under a point with iTerm2's hysteresis, the source and target looks and messages, compatibility, and the drop plan (split side or swap, tmux command). The app side is `src/app/pane_title_ui.rs` (chrome split, drawing, clicks, the title bar menu) and `src/app/pane_move_ui.rs` (the pick mode, the drop, the title bar drag, cross-window targets). The pane rectangle loses its title bar in one place (`statusbar_ui::pane_bar_split`, which every pane-rect caller already goes through), so pty sizes, hit tests and the tmux size report follow. The pick mode is app-wide (it spans every window, as iTerm2's); the per-frame title bar hit rectangles are per window and go in `swap_live`.

**Tech Stack:** as milestones 11a to 11e. No new crates and no new `windows-sys` features: `Win32_UI_WindowsAndMessaging` (`WindowFromPoint`, `GetAncestor`, `GetCursorPos`, already used at `src/app/windows_ui.rs:901`) and `Win32_Graphics_Gdi` (`ScreenToClient`) are enabled (`Cargo.toml:45-60`); winit 0.30's `Window::drag_window`.

### Pitfalls for the implementer

- `pane_bar_split` is the one place the title bar leaves the pane; do not subtract it again in `visible`, `grid_size` or the render loop, and keep `room()` comparing the new rects so every window relays out when a setting changes.
- The tmux size report must subtract title bars before `tabs_resized`, or tmux panes overflow their rects (implementer-rules tmux invariant).
- `title_bars` is per window: add it to `WinSlot`, `WinSlot::new` and `swap_live`, or clicks hit another window's bars. `pane_move` is app-wide: it must not go in `swap_live`.
- Moving a pane across windows: enter the source window before touching its tabs, end its replays, and use `take_tab` and `drop_current_window` so broadcast domains and the MRU forget the moved tab; then enter the target window.
- A tmux move is a command; do not also move the pane in Tandem's layout, tmux's layout change does it.
- Never put the pick messages or title text in `overlay` (text-bearing boxes go in `popups`).
- `CtxPending` must be built for the title bar's pane even when no other extra item applies, or the provider items vanish (`m11c-rulings.txt:161`).
- `start_pane_as` and `close_pane` must end the pick for every window and redraw them all, or stale overlays stay on parked windows.
- `Drag` lives in `WinSlot` and is swapped by `swap_live`: the cross-window hover keeps `Drag::Title` only because the source window is entered back in the same call. Never return early between the two `enter` calls in `title_drag_move`.
- Pick and drag hit tests use the full pane rect (`visible_rects`, title bar included), never `pane_at`, which after Task 3 excludes the title bar.
