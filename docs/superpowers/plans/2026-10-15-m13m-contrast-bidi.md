### Minimum contrast (NC, CM, PCP, PP, ASB)

- `NC:23-25`: brightness weights 0.30, 0.59, 0.11 (`PerceivedBrightness`, `NC:36-40`, on the components as stored, not linearized). `NC:694-744` `getComponents:forComponents:withContrastAgainstComponents:minimumContrast:`: when the brightness difference is below the minimum, move the text's brightness away from the background's by the shortfall; when that leaves 0 to 1, try the other side (`other + min` or `other - min`) and take it when it gives more contrast; clamp; then `NC:600-652` moves the color along the line to white (target above) or black (target below) until the target brightness (`p = (t - B(c)) / B(e - c)`, clamped 0 to 1). `CM:318-345`: applied first, before muting and dimming, unless `disableMinimumContrast`. `ASB:755` and `MetalRenderer/Glue/iTermMetalPerFrameState.m:1793`: block drawing characters are contrast-ineligible.
- Warning: `PCP:459-463` shows `_bwWarning1` and `_bwWarning2` when the value is at least 0.97; `PP:2834` the glyph (an emoji), `PP:3191` "Black & White Text Only".

### Harmony and marks (MS, TDH, NC, S)

### Decisions (each goes in `m13m-rulings.txt`)

- Minimum contrast works on the stored sRGB components with iTerm2's weights, applied after every other color choice in `pane_with`. Cost: none known.
- The harmonized cube is computed in sRGB even when `P3` is on (the P3/HDR ruling). Cost: slightly different colors than iTerm2 on a P3 display.
- Blinking text shares the cursor's blink phase and period. Cost: none (iTerm2 does the same).
- `P3` and `AllowHDR` are stored and shown and read nowhere. Cost: changing them has no effect (the ruling).
- `unicode-bidi` (already in the tree) lays out right-to-left text instead of CoreText. Cost: rare differences in how neutrals resolve.
- Mirrored characters come from Tandem's own table derived from Unicode's Bidi_Mirroring_Glyph data (generated once through ICU's `u_charMirror`, which is built from `BidiMirroring.txt`), checked against iTerm2's results; not a copy of iTerm2's table (ruling 13m). Cost: a Unicode version newer than iTerm2's may mirror a few more characters.
- The scrollbar style is re-read on every `WM_SETTINGCHANGE` through a window subclass, since winit has no settings event. Cost: one registry read per broadcast.
- The scrollbar style follows Windows' "Always show scrollbars" (Legacy when on, Overlay otherwise), as macOS follows its own scroll bar setting. Cost: a user who wants the legacy style with dynamic scrollbars must change the Windows setting.
- Scrollbar ticks are drawn by Tandem's scene over its own scrollbar; the two keys are read at pane start. Cost: none.
- Hover timestamps keep Tandem's 12-column edge rule. Cost: a different hot strip than iTerm2's 22-point scroller strip.

### Pitfalls for the implementer

- Apply minimum contrast after the selection and cursor colors, never before (iTerm2 adjusts the text actually drawn against the background actually drawn).
- The BLINK flag uses the last free bit of `Flags`; check no other milestone took it first, and keep `Attr::Reset` clearing it.
- A bidi order is per row content: cache it, but invalidate on any change to the row (damage), or a redrawn line keeps the old order.
- Every pointer-to-cell and cell-to-pixel path must go through the order, or selection highlights one place and copies another.
- The scrollbar and the Adjacent column are subtracted before the tmux size report; a tmux pane's grid never follows Tandem's room.
- The overlay scrollbar takes no room: toggling Windows' setting must relayout every pane (driven by `UserEvent::SettingChanged`, not by `ThemeChanged`, which never fires for it).
- Tests compute grid sizes through 13l's `margins::grid_size` and `scrollbar::room`, never with the old 4-pixel `PAD` literal.
- Mark colors from `mark_rgba` are already sRGB floats; the quad shader wants linear (`scene::rgba`); convert through `rgba` when moving to `mark_color`.
